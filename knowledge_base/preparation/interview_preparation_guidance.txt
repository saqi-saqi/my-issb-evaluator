# PREPARATION GUIDANCE FOR ISSB INTERVIEW
Source Type: Preparation Guidance (Level 3)
Disclaimer: This is preparatory advice and practical guidance, not official board regulation.

## How to Prepare for the Deputy President Interview

1. Know Yourself Deeply:
   - Your bio-data form is the interviewer's map. Review everything you wrote: academic marks, parent's profession, siblings, hobbies, sports, and memorable incidents.
   - Be prepared to discuss your greatest achievement, your biggest regret or mistake, and what you learned from it.

2. Master General Knowledge & National Affairs:
   - Stay updated on major ongoing national affairs: national budget, inflation rates, CPEC developments, energy projects, major river systems, dams, and border geography.
   - Familiarize yourself with armed forces basic structure: Chiefs of Staff, basic weapon systems, historic wars (1948, 1965, 1971, Kargil), and gallantry awards (Nishan-e-Haider recipients).

3. Communication Conduct:
   - Enter with confidence: Knock politely, ask permission to enter, walk with upright posture, greet the officer respectfully, and wait to be offered a seat.
   - Maintain natural eye contact with the interviewer (or pan smoothly across the panel if multiple officers are present).
   - Answer directly in the language you are asked (English or Urdu). If you start in English, maintain it with confidence. If you struggle for a specific word, pause briefly rather than using slang.

4. Handling Difficult Questions:
   - If you do not know the answer to a factual general knowledge question, state clearly and politely: "I am sorry, Sir, I do not know." Never guess wildly or try to bluff.
   - If challenged on an opinion, defend your viewpoint with logical arguments and evidence, but remain respectful. If the officer points out a valid counter-perspective, acknowledge its merit.
