# ISSB INTERVIEW DO'S AND DON'TS
Source Type: Preparation Guidance (Level 3)
Disclaimer: This is preparatory advice compiled from coaching and candidate experiences.

## Essential DO's
1. DO speak clearly and audibly; avoid mumbling or speaking too softly.
2. DO listen carefully to the entire question before beginning your response.
3. DO admit knowledge gaps truthfully with a prompt "I do not know, Sir."
4. DO support your viewpoints with rational arguments, data, and life examples.
5. DO maintain good posture: sit upright with hands resting comfortably on your lap or knees, avoid slouching or leaning forward aggressively.
6. DO show respect for differing viewpoints, peers, institutions, and community values.

## Critical DON'TS
1. DON'T fabricate facts, bluff, or invent fake statistics. Assessors have extensive experience identifying deception.
2. DON'T blame teachers, parents, peers, or examiners for past bad grades or failures. Take ownership.
3. DON'T use rote-learned, memorized textbook answers for personal questions like "Why do you want to join the Army?" Speak authentically.
4. DON'T lose your temper, argue aggressively, or roll your eyes under stress or teasing by the interviewer.
5. DON'T interrupt the interviewer when they are speaking.
6. DON'T fidget with your fingers, adjust your clothing repeatedly, or look at the floor/ceiling when answering.
