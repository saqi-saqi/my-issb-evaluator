# EVALUATION RUBRIC: EMOTIONAL COMPOSURE & STRESS RESPONSE
Source Type: Evaluation Rubric
Dimension: Deputy President & Psychologist
Category: Stress Response

## Definition
The ability to maintain mental equilibrium, psychological composure, and respectful engagement during rapid-fire questioning, provocative scenarios, or direct challenges to one's opinions.

## Observable Positive Indicators
- Emotional Stability: Maintains a steady, calm, and respectful vocal tone regardless of provocative or confrontational prompts.
- Non-Defensive Stance: Receives critical feedback or counter-arguments with dignity; engages constructively with the question rather than taking personal offense.
- Presence of Mind: Thinks calmly when confronted with surprise scenarios or unexpected questions rather than freezing or stammering frantically.
- Graceful Recovery: If caught in a mistake or factual lapse, admits it honestly and calmly resumes without visible despondency.

## Observable Potential Weaknesses
- Aggressive Defensiveness: Becomes hostile, sarcastic, visibly agitated, or argumentative when their statements are questioned.
- Panic / Disintegration: Loses the ability to construct coherent thoughts; freezes completely or displays extreme disarray.
- Fact Fabrication: Invents imaginary facts or false statistics out of desperation to hide knowledge gaps.
- Over-Submissiveness: Instantly abandons well-founded, truthful beliefs simply because the interviewer voiced skepticism.

## Methodological Guardrail
Experiencing stress, situational anxiety, or physical heartbeat elevation in an ISSB interview is completely normal. The critical evaluation metric is functional composure and honesty, not artificial robotic perfection.
