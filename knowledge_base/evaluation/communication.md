# EVALUATION RUBRIC: COMMUNICATION & EXPRESSION
Source Type: Evaluation Rubric
Dimension: Deputy President & Psychologist
Category: Communication

## Definition
The ability to listen attentively, synthesize information, and express ideas in a clear, coherent, concise, and respectful manner in both structured and spontaneous dialogue.

## Observable Positive Indicators
- Directness: Answers the exact question asked before elaborating or providing context.
- Coherence: Employs logical structuring (e.g., Situation -> Action -> Result, or Thesis -> Supporting Reason).
- Clarity of Diction: Speaks with understandable phrasing, appropriate pacing, and appropriate vocabulary without excessive filler words.
- Engagement & Active Listening: Listens fully to the interviewer's prompt, pauses briefly to think when necessary, and addresses follow-up nuances.
- Concrete Evidence: Ground responses in real personal experiences, specific examples, or verifiable facts rather than vague abstractions.

## Observable Potential Weaknesses
- Evasion / Circumlocution: Talks around the subject without ever answering the core premise of the question.
- Disorganization: Jumps haphazardly across disparate topics, losing the thread of the narrative.
- Defensive Monologuing: Over-explains or argues continuously in an effort to justify minor inaccuracies instead of acknowledging them.
- Incomprehensible Abbreviation: Provides monosyllabic or terse answers ("Yes", "No", "Don't know") with no willingness to elaborate when invited.

## Methodological Guardrail
A single unclear phrase or brief grammatical hesitation in English does NOT indicate low intellectual competence or poor leadership. Linguistic fluency must be decoupled from logical depth and moral authenticity.
