# EVALUATION RUBRIC: CONFIDENCE, INITIATIVE & DYNAMISM
Source Type: Evaluation Rubric
Dimension: Deputy President, Psychologist & GTO
Category: Confidence & Dynamic Drive

## Definition
The self-assurance to take independent action in unfamiliar or ambiguous environments, step forward without prompting to tackle obstacles, and sustain energetic determination despite friction or temporary setbacks.

## Observable Positive Indicators
- Proactive Initiative: Identifies needs and takes timely, constructive action without waiting for explicit orders or instructions.
- Composed Self-Belief: Maintains steady eye contact, authoritative vocal delivery, and decisive body language without arrogance.
- Resilience & Fortitude: Bounces back constructively from criticism, challenging counter-questions, or temporary failure.
- Courage Under Uncertainty: Willing to make calculated decisions with incomplete information and stand by them firmly.
- Dynamic Energy: Displays enthusiasm, mental alertness, and physical stamina throughout demanding interview probes.

## Observable Potential Weaknesses
- Passive Indolence / Inertia: Reluctant to initiate action; continuously waits for direction or seeks reassurance before answering.
- Fragile Ego / Arrogance: Masks deep insecurity with boastfulness, bluster, or disdain toward others.
- Timidity & Hesitation: Exhibits extreme meekness, whispering, inability to maintain eye contact, or nervous retreat under mild cross-examination.
- Monosyllabic Passivity: Responds with flat, non-committal "yes" or "no" devoid of conviction or independent thought.
- Quick Surrender: Abandons reasoned positions immediately upon encountering the slightest disagreement from the interviewer.

## Methodological Guardrail
Genuine confidence is quiet, grounded, and polite. It should never be conflated with brashness, loud voices, or disrespectful interruptions.
