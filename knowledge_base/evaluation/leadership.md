# EVALUATION RUBRIC: LEADERSHIP & RESPONSIBILITY
Source Type: Evaluation Rubric
Dimension: Deputy President & GTO
Category: Leadership

## Definition
The capacity to inspire, guide, organize, and support peers toward shared goals, characterized by personal initiative, moral courage, and unambiguous accountability for group outcomes.

## Observable Positive Indicators
- Taking Initiative: Steps forward naturally in ambiguous or challenging situations with constructive proposals.
- Personal Accountability: Openly claims responsibility for errors or failures under their direction ("I should have communicated the deadline earlier", "The misjudgment was mine").
- Peer Empowerment: Acknowledges team contributions by name or role; uses collaborative language ("we planned", "our team identified") while detailing their own specific role.
- Constructive Conflict Resolution: Mediates disagreements calmly by finding common ground, maintaining mutual dignity, and refocusing on the objective.
- Decisiveness: Demonstrates ability to weigh alternative options and make timely decisions rather than succumbing to analysis paralysis.

## Observable Potential Weaknesses
- Blame Externalization: Attributes failure exclusively to subordinates, peers, teachers, or bad luck ("They didn't listen to me", "The task was unfair").
- Authoritarian / Domineering Stance: Views leadership solely as giving orders and demanding subservience rather than motivating and coordinating.
- Passivity: Avoids taking responsibility even when designated as leader; waits for others to step in.
- Credit Hoarding: Takes exclusive credit for collective team accomplishments while downplaying peers.

## Methodological Guardrail
An assertive, quiet candidate who guides through steady competence and calm reasoning demonstrates leadership just as validly as an extroverted, charismatic candidate. Avoid superficial bias toward extroversion.
