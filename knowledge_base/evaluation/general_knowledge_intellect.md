# EVALUATION RUBRIC: GENERAL KNOWLEDGE & INTELLECTUAL DEPTH
Source Type: Evaluation Rubric
Dimension: Deputy President Dimension
Category: General Knowledge, Current Affairs & Critical Intellect

## Definition
The breadth, depth, and currency of factual awareness regarding national affairs, global geopolitics, defense concepts, geography, and strategic economics, coupled with the analytical capacity to synthesize facts into reasoned arguments.

## Observable Positive Indicators
- Factual Precision: Cites accurate names, dates, treaties, geographic locations, and economic metrics when discussing current affairs.
- Geostrategic Comprehension: Connects regional conflicts or maritime choke points to Pakistan's national security and economic interests.
- Multi-Perspective Analysis: Evaluates complex national dilemmas (e.g. inflation, circular debt, water security) by considering both structural causes and realistic policy options.
- Logical Articulation: Organizes answers into structured premises and conclusions (e.g. "Firstly regarding the fiscal deficit... secondly regarding structural energy reforms...").
- Intellectual Curiosity: Demonstrates genuine engagement with national and international developments beyond rote memorization.

## Observable Potential Weaknesses
- Factual Vacuity / Unpreparedness: Shows complete ignorance of basic national geography (rivers, borders), defense structure, or major treaties.
- Monosyllabic / Irrelevant Responses: Replies with "yes", "no", or completely unrelated statements when asked factual or analytical questions.
- Superficial Sloganeering: Relies on cliches, conspiratorial thinking, or emotional rhetoric rather than grounded data and strategic facts.
- Stubborn Bluffing: Fabricates imaginary figures or assertions instead of politely admitting unfamiliarity.
- Inability to Synthesize: Memorizes isolated trivia but cannot explain how events impact national security or policy.

## Methodological Guardrail
General knowledge in ISSB is not merely a test of memory; it is a direct window into candidate curiosity, mental alertness, civic responsibility, and analytical maturity.
