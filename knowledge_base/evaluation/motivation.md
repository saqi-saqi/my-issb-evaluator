# EVALUATION RUBRIC: CAREER MOTIVATION & INTEGRITY
Source Type: Evaluation Rubric
Dimension: Deputy President & Psychologist
Category: Motivation & Integrity

## Definition
The depth, authenticity, and clarity of a candidate's motivation to pursue a lifelong commission in the Pakistan Armed Forces, coupled with unwavering personal integrity and self-awareness.

## Observable Positive Indicators
- Genuine Purpose: Demonstrates intrinsic motivation grounded in service, patriotism, professional challenge, and alignment with military core values.
- Career Awareness: Shows realistic understanding of military life, hardships, field deployments, discipline, and training regimens (e.g. PMA Kakul, Naval Academy, PAF Asghar Khan).
- Personal Authenticity: Frank about personal shortcomings, humble in acknowledging areas of growth, and consistent across different interview topics.
- Active Preparation: Demonstrates genuine effort to prepare physically (running, stamina) and intellectually (news, reading, general awareness).

## Observable Potential Weaknesses
- Glamour / Status Chasing: Views the armed forces solely as a vehicle for personal status, uniform prestige, salary, or social privilege.
- Coerced / Passive Choice: Applies merely because parents insisted or because all other university applications failed ("I had nothing else to do").
- Superficial Knowledge: Has zero knowledge regarding military ranks, basic branches (Infantry, Artillery, Armoured, Logistics), or current defense responsibilities.
- Inconsistency: Expresses contradictory motivations when questioned by different angles.

## Methodological Guardrail
A candidate who acknowledges that military career benefits (security, honor, education) are attractive, alongside service to the nation, is expressing normal realistic motivation. Genuine honesty is valued over clichéd, robotic speech.
