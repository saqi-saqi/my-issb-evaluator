# EVALUATION RUBRIC: SOCIAL ADAPTABILITY & TEAMWORK
Source Type: Evaluation Rubric
Dimension: Deputy President, Psychologist & GTO
Category: Teamwork & Social Adjustment

## Definition
The ability to integrate seamlessly with peers from diverse backgrounds, contribute cooperatively toward collective group objectives, resolve interpersonal friction constructively, and prioritize group mission success over individual ego or personal prominence.

## Observable Positive Indicators
- Peer Integration & Inclusivity: Encourages quieter members to voice ideas and acknowledges peer contributions actively.
- Collaborative Orientation: Uses inclusive language ("we", "our squad", "together") and defines success in collective terms rather than solitary triumph.
- Constructive Conflict Resolution: Mediates disagreements calmly, focusing on shared objectives and common ground rather than personal blame.
- Role Flexibility: Comfortable leading when required, but equally willing to support and follow when another team member steps forward.
- Mutual Support & Empathy: Demonstrates genuine concern for team morale and assists struggling peers without condescension.

## Observable Potential Weaknesses
- Egocentric Monopolization: Dominates conversations, dismisses peer viewpoints, and claims sole credit for collective outcomes.
- Social Isolation / Withdrawal: Reluctant to participate in group dynamics; remains detached and contributes only when directly compelled.
- Subversive Resistance: Undermines appointed leaders or peer initiatives through passive-aggressive remarks or stubborn non-cooperation.
- Monosyllabic / Minimal Engagement: Answers team prompts with terse "yes/no" or indifferent responses, showing zero investment in group synergy.
- Finger-Pointing: Readily shifts blame to teammates during setbacks rather than sharing collective responsibility.

## Methodological Guardrail
Social adaptability is demonstrated through authentic peer respect, active listening, and collective mission commitment. Differentiate between healthy introversion (thoughtful, steady contribution) and anti-social detachment or arrogant self-centeredness.
