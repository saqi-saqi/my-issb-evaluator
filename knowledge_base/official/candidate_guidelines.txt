# OFFICIAL CANDIDATE GUIDELINES & CORE QUALITIES
Source: Inter Services Selection Board (issb.gov.pk/guidelines)
Authority Level: Official (Level 1)
Dimension: General / Officer Like Qualities (OLQ)

## Core Qualities Emphasized by ISSB
The official guidelines published by the Inter Services Selection Board explicitly state the key personal and intellectual attributes expected of potential officer cadets:

1. Clear Thinking & Intellect:
   - Ability to understand complex situations quickly.
   - Logical reasoning, sound common sense, and realistic approach to daily problems.
   - Ability to grasp the essentials of an issue without getting lost in trivial details.

2. Staying Calm Under Stress:
   - Maintaining emotional stability, poise, and composure when faced with unexpected hurdles, high pressure, or adversity.
   - Resisting impulsive panic reactions; demonstrating mental fortitude and emotional balance.

3. Teamwork & Co-operation:
   - Willingness to place group mission and collective success above personal ego and individual glory.
   - Supportive attitude toward peers, active listening, and constructive collaboration.

4. Participation & Energy:
   - Positive enthusiasm and active engagement in group tasks and discussions.
   - Willingness to contribute ideas and physical effort without holding back out of shyness or cynicism.

5. Honesty & Straightforwardness:
   - Moral integrity, truthfulness in self-reporting, and transparency about strengths and weaknesses.
   - Rejection of deceit, exaggeration, or artificial facades. ISSB emphasizes being authentic ("Be Yourself").

6. Self-Confidence:
   - Believing in one's capacity to handle challenges and speak with conviction while remaining humble and receptive to constructive correction.
   - Firm posture, clear eye contact, and steady voice during interviews and tasks.

7. Leadership:
   - Taking initiative when a situation demands direction.
   - Inspiring, motivating, and guiding others toward a common objective.
   - Assuming total responsibility for team outcomes, especially in times of failure or setback.

8. Communication:
   - Expressing ideas in a clear, concise, well-structured, and persuasive manner.
   - Active listening and respectful responsiveness to questions without being unnecessarily argumentative or evasive.

9. Motivation:
   - Genuine, deep-seated drive to serve as an officer in the Armed Forces of Pakistan.
   - Understanding what officer life entails (discipline, sacrifice, physical demands) beyond superficial perks.

10. General Knowledge:
    - Broad awareness of national affairs, international relations, Islamic history, Pakistan movement, geography, defense developments, and modern socio-economic challenges.
