# ISSB FOUR-DAY SELECTION SCHEDULE
Source: Inter Services Selection Board (issb.gov.pk)
Authority Level: Official (Level 1)
Dimension: Process & Schedule

## Arrival Day (Day 0 / Call-up Day)
- Arrival of candidates at ISSB Centre (Kohat, Gujranwala, Malir, or Quetta).
- Reception, document verification, allotment of chest numbers, room allotment, and opening briefing.

## Day 1: Psychological & Screening Tests
- Intelligence Tests: Verbal and Non-Verbal Intelligence Tests.
- Mechanical Aptitude Test (MAT).
- Psychological Tests (Written Battery):
  * Thematic Apperception Test (TAT / Picture Story Writing).
  * Word Association Test (WAT - 60 words, 10 seconds each).
  * Sentence Completion Tests (English and Urdu).
  * Self-Description / Biodata questionnaire.

## Day 2: Outdoor Group Tasks (GTO Day 1) & Deputy President Interviews
- Group Discussions (GD) on current social and political topics.
- Group Planning Exercise (GPE / Military Planning).
- Progressive Group Task (PGT).
- Half Group Task (HGT).
- Deputy President Interviews begin (half of the candidates interviewed on Day 2).

## Day 3: Individual Obstacles, Command Tasks & Deputy President Interviews
- Individual Obstacles (running, jumping, vaulting, rope climbing, high ditch).
- Command Tasks (each candidate acts as Commander with 2-3 subordinates).
- Final Group Task (FGT).
- Deputy President Interviews continue for remaining candidates.
- Psychologist Structured Interviews for selected candidates requiring deeper psychological probing.

## Day 4: Board Conference & Departure
- The Board Conference convenes with the President, Deputy President, Senior Psychologists, and GTOs.
- Composite profile of each candidate is reviewed.
- Candidate may be called in front of the board for a brief final conference interview if further clarification is needed.
- Final recommendations determined.
- Departure of candidates. Results are subsequently dispatched to respective Service Headquarters (GHQ, NHQ, AHQ).
