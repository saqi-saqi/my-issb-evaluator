# OFFICIAL SELECTION DIMENSIONS: DEPUTY PRESIDENT & PSYCHOLOGIST
Source: Inter Services Selection Board (issb.gov.pk/selection-system)
Authority Level: Official (Level 1)
Dimension: Deputy President & Psychologist Specifications

## The Deputy President Dimension
The Deputy President of the ISSB conducts an extensive, structured interview with each candidate.

Key Focus Areas:
1. Intellect and General Awareness:
   - Evaluates cognitive capability, logical thinking, grasp of current geopolitical/economic developments, and understanding of national interests.
   - Probes academic background, favorite subjects, and general reading habits.

2. Emotional Pattern and Temperament:
   - Tests emotional stability, presence of mind, and reactions under conversational pressure or counter-questioning.
   - Identifies whether the candidate is defensive, irritable, dismissive, or remains polite, composed, and firm.

3. Social Behavior and Adjustment:
   - Assesses sociability, relationships with family, friends, classmates, and colleagues.
   - Evaluates participation in sports, extracurricular activities, hobbies, and community engagement.

4. Moral Foundation and Expression:
   - Observes truthfulness, sincerity, humility, and willingness to acknowledge personal mistakes or knowledge gaps.
   - Evaluates communication clarity, fluency in English/Urdu, eye contact, and demeanor.

## The Psychologist Dimension
The Psychologist dimension conducts written psychological test batteries and in-depth psychological interviews.

Key Focus Areas:
1. Subconscious Tendencies and Personality Structure:
   - Unveils deep motivations, fears, aggression levels, and social orientation through projective tests (TAT, WAT, SCT).
   - Looks for internal consistency between biographical biodata, written projective responses, and verbal interview statements.

2. Motivation and Self-Perception:
   - Investigates why the candidate aspires to a military career. Is it parental pressure, romanticized glamour, economic necessity, or genuine patriotism and service?
   - Examines realistic self-assessment: Does the candidate understand their own strengths, limitations, and emotional triggers?

3. Stress Tolerance and Resilience:
   - Analyzes coping strategies when confronted with personal trauma, failure, academic setback, or peer pressure.
   - Distinguishes between healthy coping and maladaptive avoidance or externalized blame.
