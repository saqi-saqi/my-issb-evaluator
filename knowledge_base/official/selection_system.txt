# OFFICIAL ISSB SELECTION SYSTEM
Source: Inter Services Selection Board (issb.gov.pk)
Authority Level: Official (Level 1)
Dimension: General / System Architecture

## Overview of the Selection System
The Inter Services Selection Board (ISSB) is the premier human resource selection organization for the Commissioned Officers of the Pakistan Armed Forces (Pakistan Army, Pakistan Navy, and Pakistan Air Force).

The selection system operates on a multi-dimensional assessment philosophy. It is designed to evaluate candidates on merit, potential, character, and mental and physical capacity to undergo military training and develop into effective military leaders.

## The Three Assessment Dimensions
The ISSB selection board evaluates every candidate independently through three distinct dimensions:

1. The Psychologist Dimension:
   - Evaluates the candidate's inner personality, mental constitution, subconscious inclinations, emotional stability, and psychological traits.
   - Utilizes standardized psychological testing batteries: intelligence tests (verbal and non-verbal), mechanical aptitude tests, thematic apperception tests (TAT), word association tests (WAT), sentence completion tests, and structured psychological interviews.

2. The Group Testing Officer (GTO) Dimension:
   - Evaluates the candidate's behavior in practical outdoor and indoor group situations.
   - Observes spontaneous social interactions, group dynamics, initiative, physical courage, problem-solving in real-time, and natural leadership emergence.
   - Techniques include: Group Discussions, Group Planning Exercises (Military Planning), Progressive Group Tasks (PGT), Half Group Tasks (HGT), Command Tasks, Individual Obstacles, and Final Group Tasks (FGT).

3. The Deputy President Dimension:
   - Conducts an in-depth, structured, face-to-face interview with the candidate.
   - Evaluates intellect, emotional pattern, social behavior, general awareness, communication expression, moral fabric, and career motivation through direct dialogue and probing situational questions.
   - Assesses how candidates articulate thoughts, defend viewpoints under pressure, reflect on personal life events, and exhibit sound judgment.

## Independent Evaluation and Final Recommendation
- Each assessor (Psychologist, GTO, Deputy President) works independently without influencing one another during the 4-day assessment cycle.
- At the conclusion of the tests, the Board convenes for the Board Conference.
- Each assessor presents their independent assessment and grade for the candidate.
- The President and the Board evaluate the composite profile of the candidate.
- Final recommendations are made collectively by the Board.
- Crucial Distinction: ISSB makes recommendations to the respective Service Headquarters (Army GHQ, Naval NHQ, Air AHQ). The final merit list and call-up letters for military academies (PMA, Pak Navy Academy, PAF Academy) are issued solely by the respective Service Headquarters based on medical board clearances, ISSB grading, and overall merit slots.
